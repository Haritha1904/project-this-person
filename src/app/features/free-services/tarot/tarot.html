THE WORLD OF TAROT

What is Tarot?

Tarot is a system of symbolic cards that can be used for reflection, insight and intuitive guidance. Each card carries its own imagery, themes and meanings.

A traditional Tarot deck contains 78 cards. These cards are divided into two main groups: the Major Arcana and the Minor Arcana.

During a reading, cards are interpreted in relation to your question and to the other cards around them. This creates a story that can help you look at a situation from another perspective.

Tarot does not have to be about predicting one fixed future. It can instead be a meaningful way to pause, reflect and explore the possibilities in front of you.
